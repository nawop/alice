# Coolant flush

## Date
11.10.2025

## Mileage
65431km

## Category
engine

## Task performed
Flushed radiator to remove possible residue from last flush. Topped up overflow tank

## Reason
Since I could not find a coolant plug on the block when doing the last coolant flush, I decided to flush just the radiator with fresh, new coolant, just in case there might still be water or old coolant in the system.

## Procedure summary
1) Let engine warm up so thermostat opens and coolant is exchanged 
2) Drain radiator
3) Fill with new coolant
4) Topped up overflow reservoir

## Result
Cleaner coolant

## Notes
Coolant in overflow reservoir was low. CO test was negative. Need to look into that, reservoir might be cracked or headgasket leak is minimal.
