## Procedure summary
1) Added flush to radiator, let achieve operating temperature and idle after thta for 15-30 minutes
2) Drained engine radiator coolant
3) Added water and let engien achieve operating temperature, until thermostat opened an mixed fresh water with coolant in block.
4) Repeated drain and fill until water was mostly clear.
5) Added fresh coolant and drained, then repeated until coolant was solid green.
